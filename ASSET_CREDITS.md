## Unity setup

The project includes 22 selected FBX models and their textures. The materials use Unity's Built-in Standard shader with albedo, normal and available emissive maps. The prepared prefabs and materials are under Assets/EchoProtocol/Resources/EchoArt.

## Sound and music

The game includes 32 WAV audio files (2 music tracks and 30 sound effects) generated for the project through mathematical audio synthesis. No external audio libraries or samples were used. No separate distribution license has been documented for these files.
